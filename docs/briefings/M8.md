## 2. How it works

Eligible schemes (≥750 observations, first NAV ≤ 2014-01-01) are seeded-sampled to the frozen `max_paths: 30`: **344 eligible, 30 selected**. Each 12-month quarter-start window runs a deterministic monthly SIP of 10,000.00. For every scenario parameter the study reports the change in TWR / Modified Dietz / XIRR versus the correct history: median signed, median absolute, p95 absolute and threshold shares (all with n), plus failures. E9 groups XIRR−TWR by window start against max peak-to-trough NAV drawdown.

## 5. Limitations for review

- The study measures sensitivity to controlled errors, not real-world prevalence or user benefit.
- The grid scan can miss tangential roots (METHODOLOGY §7.7).
- XIRR statuses beyond `SUCCESS` are counted as failures, not silently dropped.
- E9 coverage is limited by the reference-solver domain.
